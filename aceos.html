What is an ACEO?
ACEO stands for Art Cards, Editions, and Originals, which are miniature, collectible artworks measuring exactly 2.5 by 3.5 inches (the size of a standard trading card). These tiny pieces can be original paintings, prints (editions), or photographs, created using any media, and are popular for affordable collecting, displaying in albums, or trading.
